# Asset-organized video direction

The user sends a product link. Cut reads its public webpage and asks Gemini for factual product context, three caption beats and a matching reaction. Readable product introductions proceed to assembly; greetings and questions remain conversation.

The eight-second edit has four layers: a licensed category photo with camera movement, animated text, mood-matched licensed music and an animated GIF on top. The browser assembles these assets and saves a playable video URL in the same chat. No image input, AI footage generation or GPU allowance is needed.

The current library contains illustrative category photos and curated tracks, not a live trending-audio feed. Exact product photography and trending status are not claimed. Existing Wan/LTX code supports legacy saved jobs only.
