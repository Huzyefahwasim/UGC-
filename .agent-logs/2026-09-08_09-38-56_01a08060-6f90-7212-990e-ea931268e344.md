### The task

A web app with a chat interface. One input box, a message thread, nothing more than that to start.

Someone sends a message, for example:

\> I'm building CalAI, a calorie-tracking app. Here's the site: calai.app

From that message your app should work out what the product is (read the URL and its content if you want), assemble a short UGC-style video, and drop the final video URL back into the same chat.

The chat should hold a normal conversation the way ChatGPT would. If someone says "hi", greet them back. If someone asks "what can you do?", say something like "I can generate UGC videos for you, just send me a product URL and I'll create a short marketing video." Only the product messages should trigger a render.

Use any JavaScript stack. Next.js is fine.

### What a UGC-style video is

Five to ten seconds, made of four layers:

1. Background video or photo
2. Trendy text overlay
3. Trending audio
4. GIF on top, the most important part

Not AI-generated. AI-organized. It picks and assembles the right assets.
